Sue's Big Moment

Meet Sue the Kalanchoe. Sue does not do "subtle." Sue saw every other plant on the patio putting out one modest flower each and said, "Absolutely not," then produced roughly four thousand tiny pink roses, each one ruffled like it's got something to prove.

This morning the sun crept over the fence at the perfect angle. Sue noticed instantly. She leaned into the light like a dog on a warm patio, and the whole bush started glowing as if somebody forgot the hot sauce was hot. I grabbed my phone. Sue found her angle. We have clearly done this before.

The green fella in the background is Robert. Robert is lovely, but nobody has ever photographed Robert on purpose. Robert knows this. Robert is fine. Robert is "focusing on himself."

Some facts about Sue: she needs almost no water, she thrives on neglect, and she blooms hardest when I forget she exists. Honestly, a role model. Although she has since moved on to greener pastures, maybe I neglected her too much! I give her a splash every couple of weeks and she responds with a floral parade. If houseplants had a customer review section, Sue would leave five stars and a note that just says "could be ignored more."

So here she is, entry number one of the photo diary. Not a bad way to start: a little sunshine, a lot of pink, and a plant with more confidence than I'll ever have before my first coffee. She lives on in my thoughts now.

Robert, if you're reading this, your time will come. Probably. Keep photosynthesising, buddy.

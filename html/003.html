flowers and grass

The blades of grass moved in the wind. The rustling could be felt and heard for miles. The flowers sang.

Each strand of grass carried in it a collection of life. It coursed through the veins of the stalks. Drawing energy and life from Mother Earth. As the life reached the tips of the reeds it moved into the air and sky above. Cycling into the atmosphere and beyond; sometimes it's returned back; in the form of sunlight or rain.

The dance of the flowers was a magical one and short-lived. The colors came and went each season; always returning the next. And so the cycle continued.
